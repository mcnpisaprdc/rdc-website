Quality Assurance

Editorial Excellence & Peer-Review Standards

Every manuscript published in our journals adheres strictly to rigorous international standards of publication integrity.

Double-Blind Peer Review

Reviewers and authors identities are mutually concealed throughout the review process. Each paper is assessed by at least two external subject matter referees.

PHREB Ethical Clearance

Human subject studies and clinical research must secure approval from our Philippine Health Research Ethics Board (PHREB) Level 1 Accredited Institutional Ethics Review Committee.

Anti-Plagiarism Screening

All submissions undergo Turnitin screening. Manuscripts with more than 15% overall similarity or exceeding 2% with any single source are returned prior to review.

Author Guidelines & Submission Workflow

Ensure your manuscript meets our technical, ethical, and formatting criteria prior to submission.

1 Formatting Style
Reference Guidelines

MCNP Journal adopts Vancouver / NLM citation conventions. ISAP DAWA & Admin series require APA 7th Edition. Manuscripts must be formatted in Microsoft Word (.docx).

2 Double-Blind File
Anonymization Protocol

All author names, institutions, email addresses, and self-identifying mentions in text and file properties must be stripped from the review manuscript.

3 Ethics & Integrity
Clearance & Turnitin

Include the PHREB/IERC clearance protocol code for clinical studies. A current Turnitin digital similarity report reflecting under 15% similarity must be attached.

4 Rapid Review
4–6 Week Turnaround

Editorial desk decision within 7 days. Double-blind referee feedback within 30 days. Continuous Diamond Open Access publication upon final acceptance.
