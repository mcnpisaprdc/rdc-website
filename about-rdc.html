Institutional Research Hub

One Center. Two Pillars of Academic Excellence.

The Research and Development Center (RDC) is the shared institutional arm serving both the Medical Colleges of Northern Philippines (MCNP) and the International School of Asia and the Pacific (ISAP). Operating under a unified administrative umbrella at Alimannao Hills, Peñablanca, Cagayan, the RDC drives evidence-based scholarship, oversees clinical and multidisciplinary ethics compliance, cultivates faculty and student research capacity, and translates academic discoveries into sustainable solutions that advance Cagayan Valley and the nation.

By harmonizing health sciences research from MCNP with criminological, technological, educational, and business investigations from ISAP, the RDC champions cross-disciplinary collaboration aligned with the United Nations Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs) and the National Harmonized Research Agenda.

Allied Health & Clinical Domain

Medical Colleges of Northern Philippines (MCNP) Research Wing

The MCNP Research and Development wing spearheads health-related scientific investigations that improve clinical practice, empower community health workers, and generate evidence-based healthcare policies in Northern Luzon.

Vision

The Research and Development Center envisions to be the center of scientific inquiry and program development in the institutions principally in the allied health disciplines and other relevant disciplines which advance the noble vision–mission of the school, thus contributing to national and global healthcare development.

Mission

The Research and Development Center is committed to conduct scientific and technological researches to probe existing phenomena in the allied health disciplines and develop progressive policies and responsive healthcare programs that address the mandate of the institution and community needs.

Multidisciplinary & Applied Sciences

International School of Asia and the Pacific (ISAP) Research Wing

The ISAP Research and Development wing fosters pioneering scholarship across criminology, forensic sciences, customs administration, financial management, teacher education, social work, and emerging computing technologies.

Vision

The ISAP Research and Development Center envisions to be a recognized center of applied scientific inquiry, socio-economic policy, and multidisciplinary innovation that advances institutional quality, enhances professional education, and empowers self-sustaining communities in the Asia-Pacific region.

Mission

The ISAP Research and Development Center is committed to generating rigorous, ethical, and solution-driven research across criminological sciences, business, customs, education, and computing disciplines, transforming empirical findings into progressive public policy and community welfare programs.

Research Priority Areas

Criminology & Public Safety

Restorative justice, crime mapping with PNP PRO2, correctional reform, juvenile intervention, and cybercrime defense.

Customs, Trade & Business

Port logistics, tariff administration, digital financial audits, supply chain optimization, and SME innovation.

Pedagogy & Social Development

Curricular efficacy, gender-fair education, mental health support, and community social welfare programs.

Applied Technology & Ecology

Arduino IoT sensor networks, disaster resilience along Cagayan River basin, and renewable energy adoption.

Knowledge Base & Policies

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Direct answers regarding ethics protocol clearances (IERC), thesis defenses, biostatistics consultation, registered cloud forms, and journal publishing.

Before requesting a research service

  • Ethics review: Form 012, informed consent / assent documents, survey instruments, and a signed data privacy pledge.
  • Statistical consultation: Form 011, statement of the problem, operational variables, proposed analytical tests, and a clean electronic dataset.
  • Defense forms: Use the MCNP or ISAP procedures and forms for your institution.
  • Journal submissions: See the journals portal and the publishing requirements below.

Contact RDC to confirm which requirements apply to your request, or email researchoffice@mcnp.edu.ph. Detailed procedures remain below.

Ethics & IERC Review What is the standard procedure for obtaining an Institutional Ethics Review Committee (IERC) clearance?
+

All research protocols involving human participants, health records, clinical trials, vulnerable populations (e.g., minors, inmates, indigenous groups), or community surveys must secure ethical clearance from the PHREB Level 1 Accredited Institutional Ethics Review Committee (IERC) before data collection begins.

Mandatory Protocol Submission Packet: Proponents must submit accomplished Form 012 (Ethics Protocol Review Application), participant informed consent / assent documents, survey instruments, and a signed data privacy pledge to the RDC Secretariat.

Review turnaround typically requires 10 to 15 working days for expedited protocols and up to 21 working days for full-board reviews. Once approved, an official Certificate of Ethical Clearance is issued and recorded in the master defense dossier.

Statistical Consultation How do researchers schedule a Statistical Consultation or Data Analysis session?
+

Researchers across MCNP and ISAP can avail of institutional biostatistical and quantitative analysis support through the RDC Biostatistics Unit:

  1. Accomplish Form 011 (Statistical Consultation Service Request Form) detailing the statement of the problem, operational variables, proposed analytical tests, and clean electronic dataset.
  2. Submit the request to the RDC Secretariat and settle the cashier processing fee receipt (for student academic theses).
  3. Attend the scheduled consultation clinic where institutional data analysts perform computations using SPSS, Jamovi, or R, and provide formal interpretation matrices.
  4. Receive the official Statistician’s Clearance Certificate required for scheduling the final oral defense.

Journals & Publishing What are the requirements for faculty and student authors to publish in RDC journals?
+

Manuscripts submitted to MCNP and ISAP research publications must adhere to rigorous scholarly standards:

  • Manuscript Structure: Strict IMRAD Format (Introduction, Methods, Results, and Discussion) with a structured abstract (maximum 250 words) and 4 to 6 MeSH/thematic keywords.
  • Citation Standards: APA 7th Edition for Multidisciplinary studies (ISAP); Vancouver or APA 7th for Health & Allied Sciences (MCNP).
  • Similarity Index Threshold: Must achieve a Turnitin similarity index of ≤ 15% (excluding bibliography and common quotes).
  • Peer Review: All submissions undergo double-blind peer review by qualified external and internal referees before DOI assignment and publication.

Visit the Institutional Journals Portal to explore submission workflows or email your draft to researchoffice@mcnp.edu.ph.

Utilization & Community Impact What is a Certificate of Research Utilization and how do proponents qualify?
+

A Certificate of Research Utilization is an official institutional award affirming that an empirical research output has achieved real-world application across one of our four institutional pathways:

  1. Policy Translation & Institutional Guidelines: Adopted into clinical protocols, municipal resolutions, police patrol strategies, or hospital care pathways.
  2. Community Extension & Grassroots Adoption: Utilized in community health, maternal wellness, or public safety outreach programs.
  3. Technology Transfer & Commercialization: Registered intellectual property, copyright, patent, or commercialization pilot.
  4. Curricular Integration: Embedded into syllabi, instructional modules, or laboratory training manuals.

Proponents submit their post-defense manuscript, adoption MOA / resolution, and stakeholder verification letter to the RDC Utilization Office via utilization.html.

Institutional Scope What is the difference between the MCNP Health Research Wing and the ISAP Multidisciplinary Wing?
+

The Research & Development Center functions as a unified twin-institution research hub serving both colleges:

  • MCNP Health Research Wing: Dedicated to medical, healthcare, clinical epidemiology, nursing, radiologic technology, and pharmacy disciplines.
  • ISAP Multidisciplinary Wing: Dedicated to criminology, public safety, criminalistics, customs administration, business, information technology, computer science, and teacher education.

Both wings share institutional facilities, data labs, and the Institutional Ethics Review Committee (IERC).
