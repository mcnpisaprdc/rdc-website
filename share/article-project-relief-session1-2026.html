PROJECT RELIEF: Let It Out, Lift It Up! — Project RELIEF Opens with Session on Emotional Awareness and Expression

The College of Criminal Justice Education (CoCJE) launched the first session of Project RELIEF: Let It Out, Lift It Up! at ISAP AVR 1, bringing together Level 3 and Level 4 students for an activity focused on recognizing emotions, expressing feelings, and strengthening peer support.
