Academic Synergy

The Twin Institutions of Cagayan Valley

Located on Alimannao Hills in Peñablanca, the Medical Colleges of Northern Philippines (MCNP) and the International School of Asia and the Pacific (ISAP) bring together health-sciences education and multidisciplinary learning. Their shared Research and Development Center supports collaborative inquiry, ethical scholarship, and community-responsive innovation, building on the vision of founders Dr. Ronald P. Guzman and Atty. Cristina Guzman.
