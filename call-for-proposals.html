Applicant Requirements

Eligibility Criteria & Guidelines

Please review proponent eligibility criteria and general guidelines before submitting your proposal dossier.

Proponent Eligibility

Open to all full-time and regular faculty members, academic heads, and clinical preceptors of MCNP and ISAP. Multidisciplinary teams across colleges (e.g., Nursing + Criminology + IT) are given priority in scoring.

Good Institutional Standing

Proponents must have no outstanding unliquidated research grant balances, unresolved ethics non-compliance flags, or uncompleted manuscripts from previous institutional funding cycles.

Agenda & SDG Alignment

Protocols must clearly articulate direct alignment with at least one pillar of the INNOVATES Strategic Agenda 2025–2030 and target at least one UN Sustainable Development Goal (SDG).

Fund Disbursement Tranches

Institutional grants are released in three tranches: 50% upon proposal defense approval and MOA signing; 30% upon midterm progress report; and 20% upon journal manuscript submission.

Process Map

How to Submit Your Research Proposal

Follow the official Standard Operating Procedure (SOP Stage 1 & 2) from capsule draft to defense and funding approval.

1

Download and Prepare the Proposal Dossier

Download RDC Form 002 (Capsule Proposal Application), Line-Item Budget (LIB) template, and Work Plan (Gantt Chart). Formulate Chapters 1–3 including conceptual framework, hypothesis, and methodology.

2

Secure College Endorsement (Form 003)

Present the capsule draft to your Department Head, Research Focal Person, and Academic Dean for initial quality screening and signature on RDC Form 003 (Institutional Routing Slip).

3

Submit Dossier to RDC Secretariat

Submit the complete digital packet via the Online Proposal Submission Portal below or email directly to researchoffice@mcnp.edu.ph. An acknowledgment receipt will be issued within 48 hours.

4

Technical Screening & Agenda Audit

The RDC Secretariat and Technical Review Board evaluate the capsule for agenda alignment, plagiarism compliance (≤ 15% Turnitin similarity), and financial feasibility.

5

Oral Proposal Defense & Deliberation

Proponents present their 15–20 minute oral defense before the multidisciplinary panel. The panel provides suggestions, formulates the Matrix of Corrections (Form 005), and votes on approval.

6

Ethics Clearance (IERC) & Memorandum of Agreement (MOA)

Upon integrating defense corrections, human participant protocols undergo Institutional Ethics Review Committee clearance. Proponents sign the official Research Grant MOA, initiating Tranche 1 fund release.

Rubrics & Scoring

Proposal Evaluation Criteria

Technical review panelists evaluate submitted protocols using the following institutional rubric:

Evaluation Criterion Weight Performance Indicators & Standards
Relevance & Agenda Alignment 25% Direct congruence with MCNP & ISAP INNOVATES Research Agenda 2025–2030, regional health/security priorities, and explicit alignment with United Nations Sustainable Development Goals.
Scientific Rigor & Methodology 30% Soundness of conceptual framework, appropriateness of research design, valid sampling computation, verified statistical tests, instrument reliability, and adherence to bioethical and data privacy protocols.
Community Utilization & Impact 25% Potential for real-world adoption, evidence-based policy formulation, technology transfer, patentability/copyright, or direct community health/crime prevention benefit in Region II.
Budget & Timeline Feasibility 20% Realistic work plan (Gantt chart), justifiable line-item budget (LIB), prudent use of institutional resources, and clear feasibility of completion within the 10–12 month project period.

Common Inquiries

Frequently Asked Questions on Calls for Proposals

Key information regarding proposal guidelines, funding guidelines, and technical review.

Can a faculty proponent submit multiple proposals in a single cycle?

A faculty member may serve as the Lead Proponent (Principal Investigator) on only one (1) institutional grant per funding cycle. However, they may participate as a Co-Proponent / Technical Consultant on one (1) additional multidisciplinary research team.

When is Ethics Review Clearance (IERC) required?

All studies involving human participants (patients, students, community respondents, hospital personnel), identifiable personal health data, vulnerable populations, or animal testing must secure formal certification from the Institutional Ethics Review Committee (IERC) under PHREB Level 1 standards before data collection begins. Ethics clearance is processed in SOP Stage 3 following proposal defense approval.

What expenses are eligible under the Line-Item Budget (LIB)?

Eligible expenses include: (1) Research supplies and laboratory consumables, (2) Travel expenses for field surveys or community testing within Region II, (3) Statistical analysis and data processing fees, (4) Honoraria for external defense panelists or field enumerators, and (5) Publication processing charges (APC) or journal indexing support. Capital outlays (e.g. personal laptops or mobile phones) are not permitted under institutional seed grants.

How does the DOST-CVHRDC Regional Research Fund evaluation differ from internal seed grants?

Regional Research Fund (RRF) proposals undergo competitive inter-institutional evaluation conducted by the Regional Technical Review Board co-hosted by DOST Region 02 and CVHRDC. Successful projects receive external regional grant funding up to ₱500,000, and proponents are required to present findings at regional health conferences and publish in international peer-reviewed journals.
