Research and Development Center

The Research and Development Center (RDC) of the International School of Asia and the Pacific (ISAP) serves as the institution’s hub for research and scholarly inquiry. It promotes the development of high-quality, ethical, and evidence-based studies in criminological, technological, educational, business, and social sciences to support academic excellence, informed public policy, and community development.

Through research initiatives, capacity-building activities, and collaborative partnerships, the RDC contributes to the advancement of knowledge and innovation while addressing local, national, and global issues. The center remains committed to producing relevant and impactful research that supports the vision and mission of the institution.

Vision

The ISAP Research and Development Center envisions to be a recognized center of applied scientific inquiry, socio-economic policy, and multidisciplinary innovation that advances institutional quality, enhances professional education, and empowers self-sustaining communities in the Asia-Pacific region.

Mission

The ISAP Research and Development Center is committed to generating rigorous, ethical, and solution-driven research across criminological sciences, business, customs, education, and computing disciplines, transforming empirical findings into progressive public policy and community welfare programs.

Objectives

  1. To develop awareness and evolve a research culture among the stakeholders of education in the school.
  2. To conduct qualitative and quantitative researches relevant to curricular programs of the school contributory to the realization to its general trust.
  3. To develop liberal policies and pro-active programs which shall strengthen the various agenda of the school.
  4. To contribute to the reservoir of knowledge with the end in view of improving existing practices and paradigms.

Purposes

  • To give focus and direction on research and development efforts that will address the needs of the college.
  • To serve as a guide to policy makers, funding and donor agencies, and researchers from public and private sectors to invest and ensure productivity and quality of life.
  • To help in providing evidence-based solutions to vital concerns.
  • To serve as basis in maximizing resources for utilization and minimize duplication of research efforts.

Research Process Guide

RDC Standard Operating Procedures

Follow the research process from proposal to publication. Open any required form to access the ISAP registered-forms folder.

ISAP SOP Matrix: Step-by-step procedures aligned with designated responsible roles and required submission deliverables.
1. Apply and Prepare for Proposal Defense Phase 01: Orientation & Proposal
Initial phase covering multidisciplinary research topic conceptualization (Criminology, Business, Computing, Education, Customs), capsule proposal screening, defense scheduling, and research adviser endorsement.
Required Stage 1 Official Form Files (ISAP Multidisciplinary): Direct local download (DOCX & PDF)
Form 002 (Application) Form 003 (Capsule Proposal) Form 004 (Adviser Assignment DOCX) Form 004 (PDF) Form 005 (Checklist DOCX) Form 006 (Endorsement DOCX)
Step & Action Responsible Person Requirement & Deliverables
1.1
Submit Defense Application Accomplish and submit the formal proposal defense application form, propose schedule, and verify proponent eligibility.
Proponent(s) / Research Teacher
Defense Application
  • Defense Application Form (Form 002)
  • Multidisciplinary Capsule Proposal
  • Curriculum Clearance / Enrollment Certificate
1.2
Evaluate Proposal & Recommend Panel Assess multidisciplinary proposal alignment with institutional/CHED priorities and recommend qualified defense examiners.
Program Coordinator / Dean
Academic Endorsement
  • Proposal Evaluation & Routing Slip
  • Panel Recommendation Form (Form 003)
1.3
Calendar Defense & Issue Notices Audit completeness of prerequisites, log defense date in master RDC calendar, and transmit formal notices to panel members.
RDC Secretary / Coordinator
Official Notice of Defense
  • Notice of Proposal Defense (Form 004)
  • RDC Master Defense Calendar Entry
  • Panel Invitation Letters
1.4
Adviser Review & Official Endorsement Review final proposal draft (Chapters 1–3) and sign official routing endorsement allowing proponents to proceed to defense.
Research Adviser
Adviser Sign-off
  • Signed Adviser Endorsement Sheet (Form 006)
  • Three (3) Bound Copies of Chapters 1–3 Manuscript
2. Conduct the Proposal Defense Proper Phase 02: Proposal Defense
Formal oral presentation of Chapters 1–3 before the multidisciplinary panel of examiners, evaluation of methodology, theoretical framework, panel deliberation, and issuance of the Matrix of Corrections.
Required Stage 2 Official Form Files (ISAP Multidisciplinary): Direct local download (DOCX)
Form 007 (Defense Minutes) Form 008 (Proposal Review Rubric) Form 009 (Process Evaluation)
Step & Action Responsible Person Requirement & Deliverables
2.1
Call Session to Order & Oral Presentation Formally convene defense session and deliver oral presentation of Chapters 1–3 within the allotted 15–20 minutes.
Panel Chair & Proponents
Presentation Deck
  • Proposal Presentation Slide Deck (15–20 mins)
  • Bound Manuscripts for Panelists
  • Defense Program & Attendance Log
2.2
Interpellation & Panel Evaluation Panel members examine theoretical framework, sampling design, statistical/qualitative methodology, instrument validity, and disciplinary ethics.
Panel Members & Advisers
Evaluation Rubrics
  • Panelist Evaluation Rubric Forms (Form 007)
  • Critique & Annotation Notes on Draft
2.3
Executive Deliberation & Verdict Announcement Panel confers in closed session to calculate average ratings, formulate verdict, and announce official result.
Panel Chair & Panel Members
Deliberation Result
  • Proposal Rating Sheet (Form 008)
  • Official Verdict Sheet (Passed / Passed with Revisions / Re-defense)
2.4
Collate Feedback & Formulate Matrix Record all panel suggestions systematically and formulate the Matrix of Corrections to guide proposal enhancement.
Proponent(s) & Research Adviser
Matrix of Corrections
  • Official Matrix of Corrections (Form 009)
  • Official Minutes of the Defense Session
3. Secure Field Permits & Data-Gathering Authorization Phase 03: Field Protocols
Securing formal gatekeeper permits, agency endorsements (PNP, BJMP, DepEd, LGUs, corporate partners), and participant informed consent before any data collection.
Required Stage 3 Official Form Files (ISAP Multidisciplinary): Direct local download (DOCX & PDF)
Form 010 (Client Satisfaction Survey DOCX) Form 010 (PDF)
Step & Action Responsible Person Requirement & Deliverables
3.1
Submit Fieldwork Dossier & Research Instruments File validated data-gathering instruments (questionnaires, interview guides, FGD protocols) with participant consent and risk management documentation.
Proponent(s) / Principal Investigator
Research Instruments
  • Validated Survey Tool / Interview Guide
  • Informed Consent & Assent Forms (English & Filipino / Dialect)
  • Field Risk Management & Confidentiality Plan
3.2
Research Ethics / Institutional Screening RDC ethics / review committee assesses participant safeguards, anonymity, Data Privacy Act compliance, and issues research clearance.
Institutional Ethics / Review Committee
Ethics Clearance
  • Protocol Review Checklist
  • Data Privacy & Participant Protection Pledge
  • Ethics Clearance Certificate (Form 010)
3.3
Authorize Agency Gatekeeper Transmittal Letters Execute official institutional transmittal communications requesting permission to collect data in partner organizations, LGUs, law enforcement units, or schools.
Dean, Agency Gatekeepers & RDC Director
Transmittal Permits
  • Signed Agency Transmittal Letters (Form 010)
  • Approved Gatekeeper Endorsements (PNP / LGU / DepEd / Partner Firms)
4. Validate Data & Conduct Statistical Treatment Phase 03: Data & Analysis
Quantitative statistical computation or qualitative thematic analysis, dataset verification, settling institutional treatment fees, and running certified statistical tests.
Required Stage 4 Official Form Files (ISAP Multidisciplinary): Direct local download (DOCX & PDF)
Form 010 (Satisfaction Survey DOCX) Form 010 (PDF) Form 011 (Statistical Treatment Form)
Step & Action Responsible Person Requirement & Deliverables
4.1
Field Data Collection & Raw Data Cleaning Collect data across designated target populations, encode responses, clean dataset, and perform codebook verification.
Proponent(s) / Field Enumerators
Dataset & Codebook
  • Retrieved Accomplished Survey Instruments
  • Clean Master Spreadsheet (.xlsx / .csv)
  • Variable Codebook & Scoring Rubric
4.2
Process Statistical Request & Settle Fee Submit cleaned dataset and formal statistical service request with hypotheses to RDC secretariat and settle fee at the cashier.
Proponent(s), RDC Secretary & Finance
Service Request
  • Statistical Treatment Request (Form 011)
  • Official Cashier Payment Receipt
  • Approved Chapter 3 Methodology
4.3
Execute Computations & Statistical Certification Run inferential statistical models (SPSS / JASP / SmartPLS / R) or qualitative thematic coding, and deliver certified analytical report.
Institutional Statistician / Data Analyst
Certified Analysis
  • Certified Statistical / Thematic Analysis Report
  • Statistical Output Tables & Charts
  • Statistician’s Clearance Certificate
5–6. Prepare for and Present Oral Final Defense Phase 04: Final Defense
Comprehensive oral defense of the full 5-chapter multidisciplinary manuscript, defending empirical findings, theoretical interpretations, and policy recommendations.
Required Stage 5 Official Form Files (ISAP Multidisciplinary): Direct local download (DOCX)
Form 002 (Application) Form 005 (Checklist DOCX) Form 006 (Endorsement DOCX) Form 009 (Process Evaluation) Form 012 (Self-Assessment Checklist)
Step & Action Responsible Person Requirement & Deliverables
5.1
Submit Final Defense Application Dossier File application for final oral defense with completed 5-chapter manuscript, data analysis certification, and adviser endorsement.
Proponent(s) & Research Adviser
Final Dossier
  • Final Defense Application (Form 012)
  • Complete 5-Chapter Manuscript
  • Adviser Endorsement Sheet (Form 006)
  • Statistician’s Clearance (Form 011)
5.2
Schedule Defense & Distribute Copies Confirm venue, schedule defense in RDC Calendar, and distribute bound manuscripts to panel members 5 days prior to defense.
Coordinator, Dean & RDC Secretary
Notice & Routing
  • Notice of Final Oral Defense (Form 004)
  • Panel Invitation Letters
  • Manuscript Acknowledgment Receipts
5.3
Conduct Oral Final Defense Presentation Deliver 20–25 minute oral presentation defending empirical findings, discussion, conclusions, limitations, and actionable community/policy interventions.
Proponent(s) before Panel of Examiners
Defense Presentation
  • Final Defense Presentation Deck
  • Research Artifacts / System Demo (for IT/Computing)
  • Defense Program & Attendance Log
5.4
Executive Deliberation & Matrix of Corrections Panel calculates final grade, determines official defense verdict, announces result, and itemizes required manuscript modifications.
Panel Chair & Panel Members
Deliberation Result
  • Final Defense Rating Form (Form 008)
  • Official Deliberation Verdict Form
  • Matrix of Final Corrections (Form 009)
7. Plagiarism Clearance, Final Checking & Institutional Approval Phase 04: Manuscript Approval
Verification of panel compliance, Turnitin originality similarity verification (≤ 15%), and securing institutional approval signatures for ISAP blue hardbound archiving.
Required Stage 6 Official Form Files (ISAP Multidisciplinary): Direct local download (DOCX)
Form 005 (Checklist DOCX) Form 013 (Plagiarism Processing Form)
Step & Action Responsible Person Requirement & Deliverables
7.1
Execute Corrections & Secure Verification Integrate all final panel corrections into manuscript and secure individual sign-offs on the compliance matrix from each panelist and adviser.
Proponent(s), Panelists & Adviser
Compliance Matrix
  • Signed Matrix of Corrections (Form 009)
  • Clean Revised Manuscript (Chapters 1–5)
7.2
Turnitin Originality Check Submit digital manuscript for institutional plagiarism check to confirm similarity index adheres to the institutional benchmark (≤ 15%).
RDC Secretariat / Plagiarism Officer
Plagiarism Clearance
  • Turnitin Digital Originality Report (≤ 15%)
  • Certificate of Plagiarism Clearance (Form 013)
7.3
Route Approval Signatures & Hardbound Archive Secure final signatures from Dean and RDC Director on the Approval Sheet, submit 3 blue hardbound books, and upload archive PDF with dataset.
Panel Chair, Academic Dean & RDC Director
Institutional Approval
  • Signed Official Approval Sheet (Form 005)
  • Three (3) ISAP Blue Hardbound Books
  • Archive CD / USB with PDF & Clean Dataset
8. Format Conversion, Peer Review & Journal Publication Phase 05: Publication & Archive
Converting full multidisciplinary thesis into standard IMRAD scholarly journal format, double-blind peer review, and formal indexing in ISAP Multidisciplinary Research Journal or Journal of Administration.
Required Stage 7 Official Form Files (ISAP Multidisciplinary): Direct local download (DOCX)
Form 014 (IMRAD Self-Assessment) Form 015 (Publication Processing Fee) Form 016 (Acceptance for Publication)
Step & Action Responsible Person Requirement & Deliverables
8.1
Reformat to IMRAD Journal Article Condense 5-chapter thesis into a 5,000–8,000 word publishable IMRAD article with structured abstract, keywords, and author declarations.
Author(s) / Proponents
IMRAD Manuscript
  • IMRAD Formatted Manuscript (.docx)
  • Structured Abstract (max 250 words)
  • Author Affiliations & ORCID iDs
  • Author Self-Assessment Checklist (Form 014)
8.2
Submit to ISAP Multidisciplinary Journal OJS Portal Upload manuscript to ISAP Multidisciplinary Research Journal (journal.isap.edu.ph) or ISAP Journal of Administration.
Corresponding Author & Managing Editor
OJS Submission
  • Active OJS Portal Submission Record
  • Metadata & Author Bio Statements
  • Anonymized Blinded Manuscript File
  • Author Copyright Transfer Agreement (Form 015)
8.3
Double-Blind Peer Review & Publication Undergo double-blind review, address reviewer revisions, finalize galley proofs, register DOI, and publish online.
Peer Reviewers, Managing Editor & Staff
Published Article & DOI
  • Double-Blind Peer Review Evaluation Sheets
  • Official Letter of Acceptance
  • Author-Approved Galley Proof
  • Certificate of Publication (Form 016)
  • Registered CrossRef Digital Object Identifier (DOI)
