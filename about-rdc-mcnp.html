Research and Development Center

The Research and Development Center (RDC) of the Medical Colleges of Northern Philippines (MCNP) serves as the institution’s hub for research and scholarly inquiry. It promotes the development of high-quality, ethical, and evidence-based studies in the health and social sciences to support academic excellence, informed decision-making, and community development.

Through research initiatives, capacity-building activities, and collaborative partnerships, the RDC contributes to the advancement of knowledge and innovation while addressing local, national, and global issues. The center remains committed to producing relevant and impactful research that supports the vision and mission of the institution.

Mission

The Research and Development Center is committed to conduct scientific and technological researches to probe existing phenomena in the allied health disciplines and other relevant disciplines and develop progressive policies and responsive programs which address the mandate of this institution.

Vision

The Research and Development Center envisions to be the center of scientific inquiry and program development in the institutions principally in the allied health disciplines and other relevant disciplines which advance the noble vision–mission of the school thus contribute to its development.

Objectives

  1. To develop awareness and evolve a research culture among the stakeholders of education in the school.
  2. To conduct qualitative and quantitative researches relevant to curricular programs of the school contributory to the realization of its general trust.
  3. To develop liberal policies and proactive programs which shall strengthen the various agenda of the school.
  4. To contribute to the reservoir of knowledge with the end in view of improving existing practices and paradigms.

Purposes

  • To give focus and direction on research and development efforts that will address the needs of the college.
  • To serve as a guide to policy makers, funding and donor agencies, and researchers from public and private sectors on where to invest to ensure productivity and quality of life.
  • To help in providing evidence-based solutions to vital concerns.
  • To serve as basis in maximizing resources for utilization and minimize duplication of research efforts.

MCNP SOP Matrix: Step-by-step procedures aligned with designated responsible roles and required submission deliverables.
1. Apply and Prepare for Proposal Defense Phase 01: Orientation & Proposal
Initial phase covering health & medical sciences topic conceptualization, capsule proposal screening, formal defense scheduling in the RDC master calendar, and research adviser endorsement.
Required Stage 1 Official Form Files (MCNP Health Sciences): Direct local download (DOCX & PDF)
Form 002 (Application) Form 003 (Capsule Proposal) Form 004 (Adviser Assignment DOCX) Form 004 (PDF) Form 005 (Checklist DOCX) Form 005 (PDF) Form 006 (Endorsement DOCX) Form 006 (PDF)
Step & Action Responsible Person Requirement & Deliverables
1.1
Submit Defense Application Accomplish and submit the formal proposal defense application form, propose schedule, and verify proponent eligibility.
Proponent(s) / Research Teacher
Defense Application
  • Defense Application Form (Form 002)
  • Health Sciences Capsule Proposal
  • Curriculum Clearance / Enrollment Certificate
1.2
Evaluate Proposal & Recommend Panel Assess health sciences proposal relevance to NUHHRDA/RDC agenda and recommend specialized clinical panelists.
Program Coordinator / Dean
Academic Endorsement
  • Proposal Evaluation & Routing Slip
  • Clinical Panel Recommendation (Form 003)
1.3
Calendar Defense & Issue Notices Audit completeness of prerequisites, log defense date in master RDC calendar, and transmit formal notices to panel members.
RDC Secretary / Coordinator
Official Notice of Defense
  • Notice of Proposal Defense (Form 004)
  • RDC Master Defense Calendar Entry
  • Panel Invitation Letters
1.4
Adviser Review & Official Endorsement Review final proposal draft (Chapters 1–3) and sign official routing endorsement allowing proponents to proceed to defense.
Research Adviser
Adviser Sign-off
  • Signed Adviser Endorsement Sheet (Form 006)
  • Three (3) Bound Copies of Chapters 1–3 Manuscript
2. Conduct the Proposal Defense Proper Phase 02: Proposal Defense
Formal oral presentation of Chapters 1–3 before the multidisciplinary panel of examiners, evaluation of methodology and conceptual framework, panel deliberation, and issuance of the Matrix of Corrections.
Required Stage 2 Official Form Files (MCNP Health Sciences): Direct local download (DOCX)
Form 007 (Defense Minutes) Form 008 (Proposal Review Rubric) Form 009 (Process Evaluation)
Step & Action Responsible Person Requirement & Deliverables
2.1
Call Session to Order & Oral Presentation Formally convene defense session and deliver oral presentation of Chapters 1–3 within the allotted 15–20 minutes.
Panel Chair & Proponents
Presentation Deck
  • Proposal Presentation Slide Deck (15–20 mins)
  • Bound Manuscripts for Panelists
  • Defense Program & Attendance Log
2.2
Interpellation & Panel Evaluation Panel members raise critical questions regarding clinical protocol, theoretical framework, sampling design, and health ethics.
Panel Members & Advisers
Evaluation Rubrics
  • Panelist Evaluation Rubric Forms (Form 007)
  • Critique & Annotation Notes on Draft
2.3
Executive Deliberation & Verdict Announcement Panel confers in closed session to calculate average ratings, formulate verdict, and announce official result.
Panel Chair & Panel Members
Deliberation Result
  • Proposal Rating Sheet (Form 008)
  • Official Verdict Sheet (Passed / Passed with Revisions / Re-defense)
2.4
Collate Feedback & Formulate Matrix Record all panel suggestions systematically and formulate the Matrix of Corrections to guide proposal enhancement.
Proponent(s) & Research Adviser
Matrix of Corrections
  • Official Matrix of Corrections (Form 009)
  • Official Minutes of the Defense Session
3. Secure Ethics Clearance & Data-Gathering Permit Phase 03: Ethics Review
Strict ethical clearance requirement before any clinical data collection or hospital/laboratory trials. Guarantees patient confidentiality and PHREB adherence.
Required Stage 3 Official Form Files (MCNP Health Sciences): Direct local download (DOCX)
Form 010 (Client Satisfaction Survey)
Step & Action Responsible Person Requirement & Deliverables
3.1
Submit Ethics Protocol & Instruments File full protocol dossier including validated clinical tools, surveys, patient informed consent, and assent forms.
Proponent(s) / Principal Investigator
Protocol Dossier
  • Survey Instrument / Interview Protocol
  • Informed Consent & Assent Forms (English & Local Dialect)
  • Participant Risk Mitigation Plan
3.2
IERC Review & Clearance Certification Institutional Ethics Review Committee evaluates human participant safeguards, clinical bioethics, and issues clearance.
Institutional Ethics Review Committee (IERC)
Ethics Certificate
  • IERC Protocol Review Checklist
  • Conflict of Interest Declaration
  • IERC Ethics Clearance Certificate (Form 010)
3.3
Authorize Clinical & Hospital Gatekeeper Permits Execute official administrative transmittal letters permitting researchers to conduct trials in affiliated hospitals or health centers.
Dean, Hospital Gatekeepers & RDC Director
Transmittal Permits
  • Signed Transmittal Letters (Form 010)
  • Authorized Institutional Permit to Collect Data
4. Validate Data & Conduct Statistical Treatment Phase 03: Data & Analysis
Biostatistical analysis, clinical data verification, settling service fees, and running certified inferential tests.
Required Stage 4 Official Form Files (MCNP Health Sciences): Direct local download (DOCX)
Form 010 (Satisfaction Survey) Form 011 (Statistical Treatment Form)
Step & Action Responsible Person Requirement & Deliverables
4.1
Field Data Collection & Raw Data Cleaning Gather data in approved clinical/community settings, encode responses, clean dataset, and detect outliers.
Proponent(s) / Field Enumerators
Dataset & Codebook
  • Retrieved Accomplished Questionnaire Forms
  • Clean Data Spreadsheet (.xlsx / .csv)
  • Variable Codebook & Scoring Key
4.2
Process Statistical Request & Settle Fee Submit dataset and statistical service request with hypotheses to RDC secretariat and settle fee at the cashier.
Proponent(s), RDC Secretary & Finance
Service Request
  • Statistical Treatment Request (Form 011)
  • Official Cashier Payment Receipt
  • Approved Chapter 3 Methodology
4.3
Execute Computations & Statistical Certification Run biostatistical tests (SPSS / JASP / R), generate statistical cross-tabulations, and deliver certified report.
Institutional Statistician / Data Analyst
Certified Analysis
  • Certified Statistical Analysis Report
  • Statistical Output Tables & Visualizations
  • Statistician’s Clearance Certificate
5–6. Prepare for and Present Oral Final Defense Phase 04: Final Defense
Comprehensive oral defense of the full 5-chapter health sciences manuscript, showcasing clinical findings, interpretations, and policy recommendations.
Required Stage 5 Official Form Files (MCNP Health Sciences): Direct local download (DOCX & PDF)
Form 002 (Application) Form 005 (Checklist DOCX) Form 005 (PDF) Form 006 (Endorsement DOCX) Form 006 (PDF) Form 009 (Process Evaluation) Form 012 (Self-Assessment DOCX) Form 012 (PDF)
Step & Action Responsible Person Requirement & Deliverables
5.1
Submit Final Defense Application Dossier File application for final oral defense with completed 5-chapter manuscript, biostatistical clearance, and adviser endorsement.
Proponent(s) & Research Adviser
Final Dossier
  • Final Defense Application (Form 012)
  • Complete 5-Chapter Manuscript
  • Adviser Endorsement Sheet (Form 006)
  • Statistician’s Clearance (Form 011)
5.2
Schedule Defense & Distribute Copies Confirm venue, schedule defense in RDC Calendar, and distribute bound manuscripts to panel members 5 days prior to defense.
Coordinator, Dean & RDC Secretary
Notice & Routing
  • Notice of Final Oral Defense (Form 004)
  • Panel Invitation Letters
  • Manuscript Acknowledgment Receipts
5.3
Conduct Oral Final Defense Presentation Deliver 20–25 minute oral presentation defending clinical findings, interpretations, limitations, and recommendations.
Proponent(s) before Panel of Examiners
Defense Presentation
  • Final Defense Presentation Deck
  • Clinical / Laboratory Evidence Demonstration
  • Defense Program & Attendance Log
5.4
Executive Deliberation & Matrix of Corrections Panel calculates final score, determines official verdict, announces result, and itemizes required manuscript modifications.
Panel Chair & Panel Members
Deliberation Result
  • Final Defense Rating Form (Form 008)
  • Official Deliberation Verdict Form
  • Matrix of Final Corrections (Form 009)
7. Plagiarism Clearance, Final Checking & Institutional Approval Phase 04: Manuscript Approval
Verification of panel compliance, Turnitin originality similarity verification (≤ 15%), and securing institutional approval signatures for MCNP hardbound archiving.
Required Stage 6 Official Form Files (MCNP Health Sciences): Direct local download (DOCX & PDF)
Form 005 (Checklist DOCX) Form 005 (PDF) Form 013 (Plagiarism Processing Form)
Step & Action Responsible Person Requirement & Deliverables
7.1
Execute Corrections & Secure Verification Integrate all final panel corrections into manuscript and secure individual sign-offs on the compliance matrix from each panelist and adviser.
Proponent(s), Panelists & Adviser
Compliance Matrix
  • Signed Matrix of Corrections (Form 009)
  • Clean Revised Manuscript (Chapters 1–5)
7.2
Turnitin Originality Check Submit digital manuscript for institutional plagiarism check to confirm similarity index adheres to the institutional benchmark (≤ 15%).
RDC Secretariat / Plagiarism Officer
Plagiarism Clearance
  • Turnitin Digital Originality Report (≤ 15%)
  • Certificate of Plagiarism Clearance (Form 013)
7.3
Route Approval Signatures & Hardbound Archive Secure final signatures from Dean and RDC Director on the Approval Sheet, submit 3 green hardbound books, and upload archive PDF.
Panel Chair, Academic Dean & RDC Director
Institutional Approval
  • Signed Official Approval Sheet (Form 005)
  • Three (3) MCNP Green Hardbound Books
  • Archive CD / USB with PDF & Clean Dataset
8. Format Conversion, Peer Review & Journal Publication Phase 05: Publication & Archive
Converting full medical/allied health thesis into standard IMRAD scholarly journal format, double-blind peer review, and formal indexing in MCNP Journal of Medical & Allied Health Sciences.
Required Stage 7 Official Form Files (MCNP Health Sciences): Direct local download (DOCX)
Form 014 (IMRAD Self-Assessment) Form 015 (Publication Processing Fee) Form 016 (Acceptance for Publication)
Step & Action Responsible Person Requirement & Deliverables
8.1
Reformat to IMRAD Journal Article Condense 5-chapter thesis into a 5,000–8,000 word publishable IMRAD article with structured abstract, keywords, and author declarations.
Author(s) / Proponents
IMRAD Manuscript
  • IMRAD Formatted Manuscript (.docx)
  • Structured Abstract (max 250 words)
  • Author Affiliations & ORCID iDs
  • Author Self-Assessment Checklist (Form 014)
8.2
Submit to MCNP Health Journal OJS Portal Upload manuscript to MCNP Journal of Medical & Allied Health Sciences (journal.mcnp.edu.ph).
Corresponding Author & Managing Editor
OJS Submission
  • Active OJS Portal Submission Record
  • Metadata & Author Bio Statements
  • Anonymized Blinded Manuscript File
  • Author Copyright Transfer Agreement (Form 015)
8.3
Double-Blind Peer Review & Publication Undergo double-blind review, address reviewer revisions, finalize galley proofs, register DOI, and publish online.
Peer Reviewers, Managing Editor & Staff
Published Article & DOI
  • Double-Blind Peer Review Evaluation Sheets
  • Official Letter of Acceptance
  • Author-Approved Galley Proof
  • Certificate of Publication (Form 016)
  • Registered CrossRef Digital Object Identifier (DOI)
